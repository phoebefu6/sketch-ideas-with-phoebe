# You approved the number. You inherited the shadow.

## Key concept

A forecast is a distribution, but executive reporting often delivers it as one point. The point is
the crisp object. The prediction interval is the widening shadow that the business actually has to
operate inside.

## Visual direction

- Format: staged-object poster, built as editable SVG
- Object: a small freestanding `$4.23M` point estimate
- Shadow: a measured fan widening across quarter lines
- Grid: forecast horizon
- Accent: the far commitment marker, and nothing else
- Mood: calm, forensic, expensive - evidence rather than alarm

## Takeaway

Ask for the width, not just the value. Removing the interval does not remove the risk - it silently
transfers the uncertainty from the forecaster to the operator.
