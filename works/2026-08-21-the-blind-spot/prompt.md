Cinematic product photography of a precision-machined exploded assembly, floating in a deep
near-black void. The look of a high-end industrial product hero shot: Leica-grade, editorial,
reverent, expensive.

THE OBJECT. A vertical exploded stack of five circular machined modules, aligned on one
invisible central axis, separated by generous even gaps so the assembly reads as deliberately
disassembled in mid-air. Each module is a thick precision-turned disc, roughly the proportions
of a camera lens mount: anodised graphite aluminium bodies, brushed titanium collars, black
ceramic inserts, fine concentric machining marks catching the light, hairline chamfers, visible
micro-screws and tiny milled vents.

FOUR of the five modules are SEALED and INTACT. Each has a continuous illuminated seam of cold
cyan light running around its circumference where the two halves close - a thin, precise,
confident line of light, like a sealed instrument. Their faces are solid, unbroken, machined
smooth.

THE THIRD MODULE FROM THE TOP IS BREACHED. Where its centre should be there is a clean circular
VOID - an empty hole punched straight through the disc, edges crisply machined and chamfered, with
absolutely nothing inside it. Through the hole you can see straight through to the black void
behind the assembly. This module has NO cyan seam light. Instead its inner bore is rimmed with a
narrow band of hot amber warning light, and a faint amber glow spills from the opening onto the
adjacent modules above and below. The hole is the focal point of the entire image: the eye must
go there first.

LIGHTING AND LENS. Shot on a 100mm macro at f/8. One large softbox high and to the left giving a

motes catching light. Deep specular highlights on the machined rings, true blacks in the

cold cyan, one hot amber accent. Extremely sharp, high microcontrast, immaculate.

PALETTE, strictly: near-black, graphite grey, brushed steel, cold cyan, one hot amber. No other
