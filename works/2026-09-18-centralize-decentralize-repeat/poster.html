The data-team pendulum · 1960–2026

Centralize. Decentralize. Repeat.

Sixty-six years of one swing, drawn on a true time axis. Every turn below is a dated, sourced event — and the shape they make was described in 1960, before the first data team existed.
“Top management decides that things have gotten out of hand” — and tightens up. Then the tightened thing cannot be run centrally, so it is loosened again. McGregor called it the accordion effect, and he was writing about factories.
Douglas McGregor, The Human Side of Enterprise, McGraw-Hill, 1960
You are not choosing an architecture. You are standing on a phase.
The default answer has reversed six times since 1960, and the turns keep getting closer — 15 years from the glass house to the spreadsheet, 4 from the cloud warehouse to the data mesh. So the question with leverage is not which side is right. It is: which half of the swing was I hired into, and what did the last one break?
