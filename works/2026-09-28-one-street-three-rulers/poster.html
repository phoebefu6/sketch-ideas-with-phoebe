AI adoption rate, decoded

18% or 78%?

Both are the US AI adoption rate, measured the same autumn. Nobody is wrong. They counted different things on the same street.

An adoption rate without its unit is not a number yet. Say per firm, per worker, or per worker-at-a-firm - then the date.

Why the rulers split: big firms adopt first (37% of firms with 250+ employees vs under 20% of the smallest, Census, May 2026), and big firms hold most of the workers.

SOURCES: J. S. Allen, "Monitoring AI Adoption in the U.S. Economy", FEDS Notes, Federal Reserve Board, 3 Apr 2026 (BTOS 18% of firms, Dec 2025; RPS 41% of workforce, Nov 2025; SBU 78% of labor force, employment-weighted, Nov 2025). US Census Bureau, "Large Firms With at Least 20 Employees Biggest AI Users", May 2026.
