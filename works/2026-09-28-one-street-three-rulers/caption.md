# LinkedIn caption - 18% or 78%?

18% of US firms use AI. 78% of US workers work at a firm that does. Same autumn, both true.

I kept seeing both numbers in decks, sometimes on the same slide, so I painted the street they
were measured on.

Two tall office towers fly the AI flag, full of people at screens. Around them, a baker, a barber,
a bookseller, a tailor - working by hand. One cafe owner at a laptop, no flag on her shop.

Now send out three surveyors:
- one counts the doors. Most doors are small shops, so the number is small: 18% of firms (Census).
- one wraps a tape round the towers and counts everyone inside, using AI or not: 78% of the
  workforce is at an adopting firm (Atlanta Fed).
- one climbs a ladder with a lantern and checks who is actually using it, cafe included: 41% of
  workers (Real-Time Population Survey).

Big firms adopt first, and big firms hold most of the people. Nobody here is lying. The unit is
doing the talking.

So my rule for any adoption number that reaches a leadership meeting: say per firm, per worker,
or per worker-at-a-firm, then the date. Without that it is not a number yet, it is a mood.

Which of the three surveyors is your company's dashboard sending out - and did anyone choose it on
purpose?
