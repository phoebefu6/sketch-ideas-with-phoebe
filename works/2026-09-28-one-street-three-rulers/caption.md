# LinkedIn caption - 18% or 78%?

18% of US firms use AI. 78% of US workers work at a firm that does. Same autumn, both true.

I kept seeing both numbers in decks, sometimes on the same slide, so I drew the street they were
measured on.

Ten firms. Eight small shops with four people each, two towers with sixty each. Only the towers
use AI. A few people in the small shops use it anyway, on their own - their owner would tell a
survey "no".

Now measure it three ways:
- count the firms: 2 of 10. 20%.
- count the workers under an AI-using roof: 120 of 152. 79%.
- count the people who actually use it: 56 of 152. 37%.

The real US figures for late 2025 land in the same order - 18% of firms (Census), 78% of the
workforce at an adopting firm (Atlanta Fed), 41% of workers using genAI for work (Real-Time
Population Survey) - and for the same reason. Big firms adopt first, and big firms hold most of
the people.

Nobody here is lying. The unit is doing the talking.

So my rule for any adoption number that reaches a leadership meeting: say per firm, per worker,
or per worker-at-a-firm, then the date. Without that it is not a number yet, it is a mood.

Which of the three rulers is your company's dashboard using - and did anyone choose it on purpose?
