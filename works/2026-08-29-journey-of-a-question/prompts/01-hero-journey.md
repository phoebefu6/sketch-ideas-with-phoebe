Vertical Chinese xianxia concept-art panorama, portrait. An immense pilgrimage mountain rising
through a sea of cloud, four distinct stations connected by one winding stone path climbing from
the very bottom to the snowy summit.

BOTTOM STATION: a great cinnabar-red memorial archway gate (paifang, dougong brackets, glazed
jade-green tile roof) at the mountain's foot; travelers' baskets and bundles left abandoned in a
neat row BEFORE the gate, and tiny robed figures walking through it carrying nothing.
LOWER-MIDDLE STATION: a narrow rope-and-plank bridge crossing a luminous strait of cloud-sea,
paper lanterns strung along its length, tiny figures crossing in single file.
UPPER-MIDDLE STATION: a vast many-pillared great hall built into the cliff face, hundreds of
warm lantern-lit windows, weathered bronze fittings, its interior glowing.
SUMMIT STATION: a small open pavilion alone above all cloud in snow, one tiny figure at a stone
table, first dawn light striking it.

Shafts of sun breaking through cloud, warm lantern glow against cold blue snow. Palette strictly
cinnabar red, jade green, ink black, snow white, weathered gold. Painted concept art with visible
brushwork and ink wash, no photographic gloss, no 3D render sheen. Tiny distant figures for scale
only, never prominent.

LARGE CALM CLOUD BANKS fill the left and right thirds of the frame beside each station,
deliberately soft and empty, reserved for composited annotations. A generous band of soft
undisturbed cloud across the very top edge and the very bottom edge, reserved for type.

Absolutely no lettering, no Chinese characters, no calligraphy, no signboards, no banners or
scrolls with writing, no numerals, no seals or stamps anywhere in the image - every word is
composited afterwards in HTML.
