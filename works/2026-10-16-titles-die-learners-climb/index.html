The making of

Key concept

Every few months a feed somewhere announces that data science is dying. Job titles do die. The work underneath them keeps moving, and the people who last in data and AI are the ones who keep learning whatever the next problem needs. The title was never the job. The problem in front of you is the job.

Explanation

The picture is a game map with no end point.

  • The flags are course domains, not job titles. Each one marks a field Phoebe had to pick up because the work demanded it, with the number of courses in her learning log for that field: data and analytics, data engineering and cloud, data science, leadership, business, shipping and MLOps, data and AI governance.
  • The rope bridge is a jump between mountains. Nobody plans to move from the data mountain to the AI mountain. The work moves, and you follow it across.
  • The climber is still climbing. Applied AI and AI & LLMs are where the learning is now.
  • Both summits are lost in cloud. Every summit turns out to be a ledge.
  • The fogged mountain has locked doors. The next rooms are not on any map yet. That is the part that keeps it interesting.

The numbers

296 courses across 21 domains in Phoebe's learning log. Only 16 of them are labelled data science. The counts are computed from the log, not typed by hand. Many of those courses are still in progress or on the to-do list - the map shows where the learning goes, not a pile of certificates.

Takeaway

If you are curious, hands-on and cannot leave a broken thing alone, you are a data person, whatever your title says. Keep climbing.

The summit disappears into a soft bank of white cloud.

- CENTRE-RIGHT: a second, taller mountain. High on its slope a small determined young woman climber, about one tenth of the frame height, ponytail, backpack, ice axe raised mid-swing, clearly working hard as she climbs upward. Below her on the path, one blank pennant flag. Its summit is lost in cloud.
- FAR RIGHT: a third mountain almost entirely veiled in thick drifting fog, only faintly visible, with three small mysterious stone doorways carved into its rock, each sealed by a plain door with a padlock, softly glowing at the edges like unexplored rooms in a puzzle game.

RESERVED EMPTY ZONES: keep the top 20 percent of the frame as calm, open, pale sky with nothing in it. Keep the bottom 10 percent as soft pale mist with nothing in it. Leave clear open rock or sky beside every flag so a label can sit there later.

HAND: painted relief shading in gouache and translucent watercolour washes over fine ink linework, visible brush edges and gentle pooling, hatched rock faces, the warm hand of an antique panorama painter. This is a painting, not a 3D render, not a photograph, not flat vector.

PALETTE, strictly: warm cream paper ground, slate indigo and soft blue-grey for rock and shadow, pale cloud white, and exactly ONE warm accent - the climber's jacket is vivid orange. No other orange or red anywhere, no purple, no teal, no dark background, no night sky. The overall image is light and airy.

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT ANYWHERE.
