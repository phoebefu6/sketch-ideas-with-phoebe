# Titles die. Learners keep climbing.

## Key concept

Every few months a feed somewhere announces that data science is dying. Job titles do die. The
work underneath them keeps moving, and the people who last in data and AI are the ones who keep
learning whatever the next problem needs. The title was never the job. The problem in front of
you is the job.

## Explanation

The picture is a game map with no end point.

- **The flags are course domains, not job titles.** Each one marks a field Phoebe had to pick up
  because the work demanded it, with the number of courses in her learning log for that field:
  data and analytics, data engineering and cloud, data science, leadership, business, shipping
  and MLOps, data and AI governance.
- **The rope bridge is a jump between mountains.** Nobody plans to move from the data mountain to
  the AI mountain. The work moves, and you follow it across.
- **The climber is still climbing.** Applied AI and AI & LLMs are where the learning is now.
- **Both summits are lost in cloud.** Every summit turns out to be a ledge.
- **The fogged mountain has locked doors.** The next rooms are not on any map yet. That is the
  part that keeps it interesting.

## The numbers

296 courses across 21 domains in Phoebe's learning log. Only 16 of them are labelled data
science. The counts are computed from the log, not typed by hand. Many of those courses are
still in progress or on the to-do list - the map shows where the learning goes, not a pile of
certificates.

## Takeaway

If you are curious, hands-on and cannot leave a broken thing alone, you are a data person,
whatever your title says. Keep climbing.
